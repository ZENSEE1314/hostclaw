HostClaw.ai is an AI chatbot platform that lets businesses deploy smart bots on WhatsApp and Telegram. Your bot can auto-reply to customer messages, recommend products, handle customer service, manage bookings, and send broadcast messages -- all powered by advanced AI models.
You connect your WhatsApp by scanning a QR code, just like WhatsApp Web. Once connected, the AI bot automatically responds to incoming messages using the knowledge base you provide -- your products, FAQs, business info, and pricing. You can take over any conversation manually at any time.
